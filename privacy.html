Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

Machine Learning Studio (“this site”) is an educational website published at learnvisually.fun. This page explains what data the site handles and who else is involved when you visit.

The short version

This site has no accounts, no login, and no server that stores anything about you. Every algorithm on the site runs inside your own browser. The numbers you type and the points you drag are never transmitted anywhere — they exist only in the page in front of you and disappear when you close the tab.

What is stored on your device

The site saves a single value in your browser's localStorage:

  • mls-theme — remembers whether you chose the light or dark colour scheme.

This never leaves your device and is not a cookie. Clearing your browser storage removes it.

Third parties

Hosting. The site is served by Vercel, which processes standard server request data such as IP address and user agent in order to deliver pages and protect against abuse. See the Vercel Privacy Policy.

Fonts and libraries. Mathematical notation is rendered with KaTeX, loaded from the jsDelivr CDN. Requesting that file exposes your IP address to jsDelivr, as any web request does.

Advertising

This site may display advertising supplied by Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices.

For more on how Google uses data when you use its partners' sites, see Google's partner-sites notice.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Doing so will not affect any of the interactive lessons on this site, because none of them depend on cookies.

Children

This site is aimed at students and general readers and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “last updated” date above will change with it.
